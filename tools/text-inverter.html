What is Case Inversion?

Case inversion swaps every uppercase letter to lowercase and every lowercase letter to uppercase. Numbers and symbols remain unchanged. Perfect for fixing text typed with caps lock on, or creating stylized text effects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with all languages?

Yes, it works with any Unicode letters that have case distinctions. Accented characters are properly handled.

What about numbers and symbols?

Numbers, punctuation, and symbols remain unchanged. Only letters are inverted.
