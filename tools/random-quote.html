What is a Random Quote Generator?

This tool displays a random quote from a curated collection of famous, motivational, and humorous sayings. Perfect for daily inspiration, social media posts, or simply to brighten your day.

How to Use

Select a category (optional) and the number of quotes you want (1–10). Click "Generate Quotes" to get a fresh batch. Copy the result with one click.
