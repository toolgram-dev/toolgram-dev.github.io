Why Use Random Sentences?

Random sentences help designers and developers visualize how real content will appear in layouts. Unlike repetitive lorem ipsum, varied sentences better simulate natural language patterns, helping identify spacing issues, line breaks, and readability problems before actual content is ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are sentences generated?
Our generator uses grammar templates with noun, verb, and adjective arrays. Each sentence follows proper English structure (subject-verb-object) but with randomized word choices.
Is this better than lorem ipsum?
Lorem ipsum is great for Latin-style placeholder text. Our generator creates English-like sentences that better represent real content length and word patterns for modern designs.
Can I use these sentences commercially?
Yes! All generated text is original and royalty-free. You can use it in mockups, prototypes, and client presentations without attribution.
