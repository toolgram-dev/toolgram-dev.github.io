Why Convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is a lossless format that supports transparency, making it ideal for logos, graphics, and images that require crisp edges. Converting JPG to PNG preserves quality and allows you to add transparent backgrounds. Our converter works entirely in your browser—your images never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PNG support transparency?
Yes, PNG supports transparent backgrounds. When you convert JPG to PNG, the background remains white by default, but you can edit it in image editors to add transparency.
Will I lose quality?
PNG is lossless, so no quality is lost during conversion. However, JPGs are already compressed, so the output will be the same quality as the original JPG.
Is my image secure?
Absolutely. All conversion happens in your browser. Your image is never uploaded to any server.
