Percentage Change Formula

Percentage change = ((New - Original) / Original) × 100%. Positive values = increase, negative = decrease. Used for business metrics, stock market, growth analysis, and statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate percent increase?

Subtract original from new, divide by original, multiply by 100. Example: (1250-1000)/1000 × 100 = 25% increase.

What if original is zero?

Percentage change undefined when original is zero. Use absolute change instead.
