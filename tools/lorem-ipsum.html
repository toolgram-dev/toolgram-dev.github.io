What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy text used in the printing and typesetting industry. It has been the industry's standard dummy text since the 1500s. Designers and developers use it to demonstrate the visual form of a document or typeface without relying on meaningful content. The text is derived from Latin literature, making it look like readable English without being distracting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum prevents "meaningful content" from distracting viewers. It lets clients focus on design elements rather than reading actual text.

Where does it come from?

It comes from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil) by Cicero, written in 45 BC.

Is it readable English?

No, it's modified Latin. It looks like English but doesn't form coherent sentences, which is perfect for placeholder text.
