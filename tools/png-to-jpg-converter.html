Why Convert PNG to JPG?

JPG files are generally smaller than PNGs, making them ideal for websites and email. Converting PNG to JPG reduces file size significantly, but it's a lossy process, meaning some image quality is sacrificed. This tool uses HTML5 canvas to perform the conversion directly in your browser—your files never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparency?
JPG does not support transparency. Any transparent areas in your PNG will be filled with white in the resulting JPG.
Will I lose image quality?
Yes, JPG is a lossy format. Higher quality settings (like 90%) minimize visible loss while still reducing file size. Our tool lets you control the quality.
Is it safe to upload my images?
Yes, the conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your image is not uploaded to any server, ensuring privacy and security.
