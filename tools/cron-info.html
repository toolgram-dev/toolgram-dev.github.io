What is Cron?

Cron is a time-based job scheduler in Unix-like systems. The cron expression uses five fields: minute (0-59), hour (0-23), day of month (1-31), month (1-12), and day of week (0-7 where 0/7 = Sunday). Special characters include * (any), / (step), , (list), and - (range).

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "*/5" mean?
Step value meaning "every 5 units". In minute field: every 5 minutes. In hour field: every 5 hours. The parser uses modulo logic to determine when the condition is true.
How do I run something at 2:30 PM daily?
Use "30 14 * * *". 30 minutes past the 14th hour (2 PM). The parser explains this as "At 14:30 every day".
What's the difference between 0 and 7 for Sunday?
Both represent Sunday. Some systems use 0-6 (0=Sunday), others use 1-7 (7=Sunday). Our parser accepts both formats.
