What is a QR Code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a type of matrix barcode that stores information. QR codes can contain URLs, text, contact information, WiFi credentials, and more. They're scanned by smartphones and QR readers to quickly access the encoded data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I store in a QR code?
You can store URLs, plain text, WiFi network details (SSID + password), contact information (vCard), email addresses, phone numbers, SMS messages, and more.
Are QR codes free to generate?
Yes, QR code generation is completely free. Our tool creates static QR codes with no expiration date.
Can I change QR code colors?
Yes, you can customize the dark and background colors. Make sure there's enough contrast for scanners to read the code properly.
