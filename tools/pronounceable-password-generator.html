What is a Pronounceable Password?

Most password generators create random strings like "k8#Lp2!vQ" which are hard to remember. Pronounceable passwords use consonant-vowel patterns to create passwords that sound like real words, making them easier to remember while maintaining security.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are pronounceable passwords secure?
Yes! While they have slightly less entropy than completely random passwords, they are still extremely secure. A 12-character pronounceable password with numbers and symbols is very difficult to crack.
How do you generate pronounceable passwords?
We use consonant-vowel patterns (like CVCV). Consonants and vowels are randomly selected from sets, creating natural-sounding syllables. Adding numbers and symbols increases security.
Can I use these for important accounts?
Absolutely. These passwords are strong enough for email, banking, and other important accounts. The added bonus is you can actually remember them.
