Why Use Strong Passwords?

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against unauthorized access. A good password should be long, include a mix of character types, and avoid dictionary words or personal information. Our generator creates truly random passwords that are difficult to crack.

Frequently Asked Questions

How secure are these passwords?
Our generator uses JavaScript's Math.random() which is sufficient for most purposes. For maximum security, consider using a dedicated password manager with cryptographic random generators.
What password length should I use?
12-16 characters is recommended for most uses. For highly sensitive accounts, use 20+ characters.
Are these passwords stored anywhere?
No, passwords are generated locally in your browser and never sent to any server. They disappear when you close the page.
