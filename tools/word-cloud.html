What is a Word Cloud?

A word cloud (or tag cloud) visualizes text data where word size represents frequency. Our analyzer uses frequency distribution logic to count word occurrences, filters out common stop words, and returns weighted data ready for visualization. Perfect for content analysis, SEO keyword research, and survey response analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are stop words?
Common words like "the", "is", "at", "which" that are filtered out because they appear frequently but carry little meaning. Removing them reveals important keywords.
How is word size determined?
Size is proportional to frequency. The most frequent word gets max size, others scale relative to it. This visual emphasis helps identify dominant themes.
Can I use this for SEO?
Yes! Analyze competitor content to find keyword frequencies. Identify which terms they emphasize most. The weighted list shows potential keywords for your own content strategy.
