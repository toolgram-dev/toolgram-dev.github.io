How to Use Text Replacer

Enter your text, specify the word/phrase to find and what to replace it with. Choose case sensitivity or whole word matching for precise control. Perfect for editing documents, cleaning data, or batch text replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "whole word only" mean?
It replaces only complete words, not parts of words. For example, replacing "cat" with "dog" would change "cat" but not "catalog".
Can I replace multiple different words at once?
For multiple replacements, you can use our Batch Replace tool. This tool handles one find/replace pair at a time.
Is my text stored anywhere?
No, all processing happens in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
