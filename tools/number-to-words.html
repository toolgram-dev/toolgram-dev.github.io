Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HEX color?
HEX colors are six-digit hexadecimal numbers representing RGB values. They're widely used in web design, CSS, and graphic design to define colors precisely.
How are random colors generated?
Our generator creates random combinations of RGB values (0-255) and converts them to HEX format. This ensures millions of unique color possibilities.
Can I save my favorite colors?
Currently, you can copy colors individually. For saving palettes, use our Color Palette Generator tool which offers saving features.
