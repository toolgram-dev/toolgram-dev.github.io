Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal?
Yes, YouTube thumbnails are publicly accessible images. Downloading them for personal use is generally acceptable. For commercial use, respect copyright and content ownership.
Why are some qualities not available?
Not all videos have all thumbnail sizes. Older videos may only have lower quality versions. The tool shows all available options.

No, we can only access thumbnails from public YouTube videos. Private videos are not accessible.
