How Age Calculator Works

Enter your birthdate and optionally choose a target date (defaults to today). The calculator instantly shows your exact age in years, months, and days. It also calculates total days, total months, and days until your next birthday. All calculations are precise and account for leap years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it account for leap years?
Yes, the calculator automatically accounts for leap years in all date calculations.
What if I leave "As of date" blank?
It defaults to today's date automatically.
What does "Include today" mean?
When checked, the calculation includes the current day. When unchecked, it calculates complete years/months/days only.
