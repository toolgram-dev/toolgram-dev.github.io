Why Count Text Length?

Knowing text length is essential for SEO meta descriptions, social media posts, essay writing, and programming. Our calculator provides instant statistics including characters (with and without spaces), words, sentences, lines, and paragraphs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are words counted?
Words are counted by splitting text on spaces. Multiple spaces are handled correctly.
How are sentences counted?
Sentences are counted based on periods, exclamation marks, and question marks.
What counts as a paragraph?
Paragraphs are separated by one or more empty lines.
