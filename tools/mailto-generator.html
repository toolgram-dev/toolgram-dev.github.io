What is a Mailto Link?

A mailto link is an HTML link that opens the user's default email client with pre-filled fields: recipient, subject, CC, BCC, and body. It uses URI scheme formatting (RFC 6068) and is essential for contact pages, support buttons, and feedback forms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add line breaks in body?
Use %0A for line breaks. Our generator automatically encodes text properly. For example: "Line1%0ALine2" creates two lines in the email body.
Will mailto links work on mobile?
Yes! Mobile devices open the default mail app (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail). On desktop, it opens the system's default email client.
Can I add multiple recipients?
Separate multiple email addresses with commas in the To, CC, or BCC fields. The generator properly encodes commas for the mailto syntax.
