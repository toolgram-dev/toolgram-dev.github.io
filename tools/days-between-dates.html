How to Calculate Days Between Dates

Select a start date and end date, choose whether to include or exclude the selected dates, and click Calculate. The tool instantly shows total days, weeks, months, hours, and minutes between the dates. Perfect for project planning, event counting, or calculating durations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it account for leap years?
Yes, the calculator automatically accounts for leap years in all date calculations.
What's the difference between include modes?
"Include both" counts from start date to end date inclusive. "Exclude both" counts between dates without counting the endpoints. Other modes include only one endpoint.
Can I calculate future dates?
Yes, you can select any dates in the future or past. The calculator works for any valid dates.
