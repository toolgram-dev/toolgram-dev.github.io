How to Crop Images

Upload any image, select crop area by dragging the crop box, choose aspect ratio, and click crop. Your cropped image is ready to download. All processing happens in your browser—your images never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop to exact dimensions?
Currently, our tool supports aspect ratio cropping. For exact pixel dimensions, you can use the custom crop and then resize in another tool.
Is there a file size limit?
For best performance, keep images under 10MB. Very large images may cause slow response in the browser.
Will I lose quality?
No, cropping doesn't reduce quality. The cropped image maintains the original resolution of the selected area.
