How to Sort Lines

Simply paste your lines (one per line), choose ascending or descending order, and click Sort. The tool automatically arranges your lines alphabetically. Perfect for organizing lists, cleaning up data, or preparing sorted output for programming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it remove blank lines?
Blank lines are preserved in their position. To remove them, use our Duplicate Line Remover tool.
Can I sort numbers?
Yes, numbers are sorted alphabetically as strings. For numerical sorting, consider using a dedicated tool.
What does "Ignore case" do?
When enabled, uppercase and lowercase letters are treated the same for sorting (e.g., 'Apple' and 'apple' are considered equal).
