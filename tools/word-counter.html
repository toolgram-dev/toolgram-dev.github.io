Why Word Count Matters

Word and character counting is essential for writers, students, and content creators. Meet essay requirements, optimize social media posts, and track writing progress.

FAQ

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting text on whitespace. Hyphenated words count as one word.

Are spaces counted?

Yes, spaces count as characters. We show both with and without spaces totals.
