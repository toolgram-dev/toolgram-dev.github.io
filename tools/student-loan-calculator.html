What is a Student Loan Calculator?

A student loan calculator helps you estimate the monthly payments, total interest costs, and payoff timeline for your student loans. Whether you have federal or private student loans, this tool provides a clear picture of your financial obligations.

Student loans typically have fixed interest rates and repayment terms ranging from 5 to 30 years. The calculator uses the standard amortization formula to compute your monthly payment based on the loan principal, interest rate, and term length.

How to Use This Calculator

Step 1: Enter your total loan amount (the principal you borrowed).
Step 2: Input your annual interest rate (APR).
Step 3: Select your loan term in years.
Step 4: Optionally add any extra monthly payment you plan to make.
Step 5: Click "Calculate" to see your monthly payment, total interest, and payoff time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average student loan interest rate?
Federal student loan rates for undergraduates are typically between 4-6%, while private loans can range from 3-13% depending on creditworthiness.
How is student loan interest calculated?
Student loan interest is typically calculated daily based on your outstanding principal. The daily interest rate is your annual rate divided by 365.
Should I pay extra toward my student loans?
If you have high-interest loans (above 6%) and no higher-priority debt, making extra payments can save significant interest over time.
Can I pay off my student loans early?
Yes, most student loans can be paid off early without prepayment penalties. Use the extra payment field to see how much you could save.
