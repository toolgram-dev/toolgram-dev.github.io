Why Analyze Email Headers?

Email headers contain a wealth of information about an email's journey, including the servers it passed through, authentication results (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and spam scores. Analyzing headers helps identify spoofing, diagnose delivery issues, and understand email security.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SPF?
Sender Policy Framework (SPF) allows the domain owner to authorize which servers can send emails on their behalf.
What is DKIM?
DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) provides a digital signature to verify an email was not tampered with during transit.
