What is Un-slugifying?

URL slugs are machine-friendly (my-awesome-post). Un-slugifying converts them back to human-readable titles (My Awesome Post) for admin dashboards, content management, and display purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

Replaces hyphens with spaces and capitalizes each word. Optional number removal cleans up dated slugs.

Can it handle underscores?

Yes, underscores and hyphens are both converted to spaces. Common in WordPress and other CMS platforms.
