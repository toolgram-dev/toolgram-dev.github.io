How to Pick Colors from Images

Upload any image, then click anywhere on the image to pick the exact color. We'll show you HEX, RGB, and HSL values instantly. Perfect for designers, developers, and anyone who needs to match colors from photos, logos, or artwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the color picking?
We pick the exact pixel color where you click. The accuracy is pixel-perfect, limited only by your screen and image resolution.
Can I pick colors from any image?
Yes, any image format that browsers support (JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WebP) works perfectly.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No, all processing happens in your browser. Your image never leaves your device.
