What is Color Palette Generation?

Color harmony is the theory of combining colors in a way that is pleasing to the eye. Our generator uses color wheel mathematics to create professional palettes from any seed color. Complementary colors sit opposite each other, triadic forms a triangle, and analogous uses adjacent colors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are complementary colors calculated?

By rotating the hue by 180 degrees on the color wheel. This creates maximum contrast and visual impact.

What's a triadic color scheme?

Three colors evenly spaced (120 degrees apart) on the color wheel, offering vibrant contrast while maintaining balance.
