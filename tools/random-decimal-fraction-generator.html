Why Generate Random Fractions?

Random decimal fractions are fundamental to statistical sampling, Monte Carlo simulations, probability experiments, and machine learning data splits. They provide uniform distribution between intervals, essential for unbiased sampling and mathematical modeling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the distribution of generated numbers?
By default, numbers are uniformly distributed between min and max. Each value has equal probability, ideal for statistical sampling and Monte Carlo methods.
How precise are the fractions?
You can set decimal places from 0-10. More decimal places mean higher precision but also more digits. 4-6 decimal places is usually sufficient for most applications.
Can I generate percentages?
Yes! Select "0 to 100" interval to generate random percentages. They work the same way as fractions but scaled to 0-100 range.
