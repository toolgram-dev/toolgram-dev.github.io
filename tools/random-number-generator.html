How to Use Random Number Generator

Set your desired range (minimum to maximum), choose how many numbers you need, and click Generate. You can allow duplicates or get unique numbers, and even sort the results. Perfect for contests, educational purposes, or statistical sampling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I uncheck "Allow duplicates"?
The generator will ensure all numbers are unique. If the range is smaller than the count requested, it will generate as many unique numbers as possible.
Are the numbers truly random?
The generator uses JavaScript's Math.random() which is sufficiently random for most non-cryptographic purposes.
Can I generate negative numbers?
Yes, you can set negative values in the "From" field to generate negative random numbers.
