Why Clean Your Text?

Messy text with extra spaces, tabs, and blank lines can be hard to read and process. Cleaning it up makes it more readable and ready for publishing, data entry, or further analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Trim extra spaces" do?
It replaces multiple consecutive spaces with a single space, and removes spaces at the beginning and end of lines.
What counts as an empty line?
Lines that contain only spaces or nothing at all are considered empty and will be removed.
